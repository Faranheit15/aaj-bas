# Product Constitution

This document is binding for Aaj, Bas. product and engineering work. It condenses the governing commitments in the [PRD](PRD.md).

1. **The edition ends.** Every edition has a genuine finite ending; no infinite scroll, endless pagination, hidden backlog, or post-completion recommendations.
2. **Agency comes first.** Do not use artificial urgency, streaks, forced timers, punitive wellbeing mechanics, guilt, locked screens, autoplay, or attention monetization.
3. **Engagement is not the goal.** More time, pages, opens, or repeat sessions are not automatically better outcomes.
4. **No behavioral ranking.** Reading behavior, profiles, clicks, and dwell time must not determine story importance or ordering.
5. **Trust outranks engagement.** Context, source transparency, reporting labels, uncertainty, corrections, and auditable history matter more than consumption.
6. **Prefer better context over more content.** Do not add volume or a continuation surface when clarity and provenance would better serve the reader.
7. **Avoid content obligations.** Do not introduce unread counts, saved-content queues, daily tasks, or other accumulating pressure without explicit approval.
8. **Keep v1 local and private.** Reading progress and preferences stay on-device in v1; do not add accounts or transmit behavioral data without an approved milestone.
9. **Automation may assist, not invent.** Generated content is untrusted, must validate against supporting sources, and requires human review until an approved milestone changes that gate.
10. **Keep architecture intentional.** New dependencies, product-scope expansion, persistence, infrastructure, or material contract changes require a documented architecture decision where required by repository policy.

When a proposed change conflicts with these commitments, choose the smallest compliant alternative or request an explicit human decision.
