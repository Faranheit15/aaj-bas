# Product Constitution

This document is binding for Aaj, Bas. product and engineering work. It is the single canonical statement of the product's governing commitments.

`AGENTS.md` section 3 lists the concrete engineering prohibitions that follow from these rules. The PRD points here rather than restating them. Change a commitment here, not in either of those.

1. **The edition ends.** Every edition has a genuine finite ending; no infinite scroll, next-page feed, endless pagination, hidden backlog, or post-completion recommendations.
2. **The app respects agency.** Do not use artificial urgency, streaks, forced timers, forced breathing exercises, punitive wellbeing mechanics, guilt, locked screens, or autoplay.
3. **Engagement is not the goal.** More time, pages, opens, or repeat sessions are not automatically better outcomes.
4. **No behavioral ranking.** Reading behavior, profiles, clicks, and dwell time must not determine story importance or ordering.
5. **Trust is a feature.** Every story exposes its sources, generation time, update history, and reporting type. Context, source transparency, uncertainty, corrections, and auditable history matter more than consumption.
6. **Prefer better context over more content.** Do not add volume or a continuation surface when clarity and provenance would better serve the reader.
7. **Avoid content obligations.** Do not introduce unread counts, saved-content queues, daily tasks, or other accumulating pressure without explicit approval.
8. **No account before value.** The complete v1 works without authentication. Reading progress and preferences stay on-device in v1; do not add accounts or transmit behavioral data without an approved milestone.
9. **No unnecessary platform.** Ship a PWA before native applications.
10. **Content is versioned.** Published editions and corrections are auditable.
11. **Automation may assist; it may not invent.** A generated statement must be supported by supplied source material. Generated content is untrusted, must validate against supporting sources, and requires human review until an approved milestone changes that gate.
12. **No attention monetization.** No ads, sponsored story insertion, autoplay, urgency colors, or notification spam.
13. **Scope expands only after evidence.** New features require a measured user problem and a written architecture decision. New dependencies, product-scope expansion, persistence, infrastructure, and material contract changes also require a documented architecture decision.

When a proposed change conflicts with these commitments, choose the smallest compliant alternative or request an explicit human decision.
